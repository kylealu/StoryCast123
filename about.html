Our mission

StoryCast exists to make travel storytelling accessible to everyone, regardless of how they read, see, hear, or navigate the web. Every story on this site is built so it can be experienced in more than one way — through video, through audio, or through text.

How we approach accessibility

Every video on StoryCast includes captions, even when there is no spoken dialogue, because captions can describe ambient sound and context, not just speech. Every story also includes a full text transcript, so the content is available to anyone who cannot use audio or video at all.

The site is built to be fully usable with a keyboard alone. Every link and button can be reached using the Tab key, and the element currently in focus is always clearly outlined. A "skip to main content" link lets keyboard users bypass the navigation menu on every page.

Text and background colors throughout the site meet the WCAG AA contrast standard, meaning text stays readable for users with low vision or color blindness.
