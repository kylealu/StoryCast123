Transcript — "Voices from the Coast"

This story contains no spoken dialogue. The audio track is an ambient recording
made along a quiet beach town: waves arriving steadily on the shore, distant
gulls, and faint footsteps on sand. The recording runs for approximately one
minute and is intended to capture the atmosphere of an early evening walk
along the coast.
